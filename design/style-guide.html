9. Rules

Always

  • White first, mint second, violet third.
  • 2px grid; sprites only at whole-number scales.
  • 1px ink outline + soft gray offset shadow, zero blur.
  • Font sizes 22 / 33 / 44px; icons are pixel sprites.

Never

  • Blending gradients, transparency, blur, border-radius.
  • Non-integer sprite scaling (mixels).
  • Copying characters, art or logos from any reference.
